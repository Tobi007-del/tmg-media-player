It is completely free to use for personal projects, open-source applications, educational use, and commercial entities with less than **$100,000 USD** in annual gross revenue.

If your company exceeds $100,000 USD in annual gross revenue, charges subscription fees or paywalls for media access, or requires enterprise support, you must obtain a TMG Commercial License.

## Commercial License Tiers

### 1. Starter Commercial
**Price:** $499 USD / year
* **Target:** Profitable indie products, early-stage funded startups, e-commerce shops, and single production web/mobile apps ($100k to $500k annual revenue).
* **Includes:**
  * Legal waiver of the $100,000 revenue cap.
  * Commercial deployment rights for 1 production application or web domain.
  * Access to all minor and patch updates for 12 months.
  * Standard email support.

### 2. Pro SaaS
**Price:** $1,499 USD / year
* **Target:** Established SaaS products, video platforms, paid membership sites, and growth-stage mobile apps.
* **Includes:**
  * Commercial deployment rights for up to 3 production applications or web domains.
  * Waiver of paywall and subscription revenue restrictions.
  * Priority issue triage on GitHub.
  * All updates and new plug releases for 12 months.
  * Fast email support turnaround.

### 3. Enterprise
**Price:** Custom (Starting at $3,500 USD / year)
* **Target:** Media publishers, streaming networks, large corporate platforms, or high-volume custom architectures.
* **Includes:**
  * Unlimited production applications and web domains.
  * Private Discord or Slack channel with the core maintainer.
  * Architectural consultation for custom tech engines and native mobile bridges.
  * Custom invoice and vendor procurement support.
  * Option for perpetual fallback license.
